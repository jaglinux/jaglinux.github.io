Jagadish Krishnamoorthy

PyTorch engineer at AMD, San Francisco Bay Area, focused on making large-scale AI workloads run efficiently on AMD GPUs. 90+ merged pull requests across PyTorch and TorchAO, spanning low-precision numeric kernels, operator implementation, and distributed training infrastructure. Served on the PyTorch Conference 2026 Program Committee (North America and Europe) and presented research on Group GEMM at the conference.

  • Low-precision quantization and GEMM: Core contributor to PyTorch's FP8 and MX (OCP Microscaling) support on AMD hardware — scaled_mm kernels, tensorwise and blockwise scaling, pre-swizzled MX FP8/FP4 scale layouts for gfx950 (MI350) and gfx1250. Contributed MX FP8/FP4 swizzle kernels and FP8 quantization to TorchAO. This work directly enables efficient inference for quantized LLMs on AMD GPUs.

  • Group GEMM / Mixture of Experts: Authored PyTorch's Group GEMM operator for MoE models (#166334), built on AMD's Composable Kernel library. Covers all 2D/3D tensor dimension combinations, hipBLASLt fallback, FP8 scaled group GEMM, and a specialized kernel for uniform-K batches. Directly relevant to MoE architectures such as Mixtral and DeepSeek running on AMD hardware.

  • ROCm backend ownership: Deep contributor to PyTorch's ROCm backend — hipBLASLt workspace tuning, CK-Tile GEMM integration, rocBLAS overloads, Magma, and architecture onboarding for new AMD GPUs (gfx950, gfx1250, gfx90a, gfx115x). Unified GEMM and SDPA architecture gating, deduplicated scaling paths across CUDA and ROCm, and maintained ROCm CI health across releases.

  • Distributed training: Contributions to torch.distributed, c10d, and RCCL — enabled and hardened ROCm distributed CI across DDP, sharded tensor, and multi-process test suites. Multi-node, multi-GPU training and inference of LLMs (GPT, LLaMA, BERT) on AMD hardware using PyTorch, DeepSpeed, and HuggingFace Transformers.

  • Ecosystem and maintenance: Maintains upstream PyTorch, release branches, and the ROCm PyTorch fork. Contributed ROCm compatibility fixes across ONNX Runtime, DeepSpeed, and Apex. Experience with multi-node HPC clusters using Slurm and Kubernetes for large-scale AI jobs.
